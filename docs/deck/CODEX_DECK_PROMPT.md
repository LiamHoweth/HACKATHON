## 4. Key facts to use (already extracted; verify against the files, then use)

**Product.** Still Here is a conversation-first AAC web app (Next.js, Safari on iPad, landscape) for adults who know what they want to say but for whom speaking or typing is slow. Rule: *AI offers possible words; the person decides what to say.* Built solo by Liam Howeth (OU computer engineering) in one day at SASEHacks for Good (hacking from 09:00 CT; first app commit `bb16883`), with Claude Code writing code under his direction (up to 15 parallel agents with exclusive file ownership, plus independent review agents whose findings got regression tests).

**Interaction loop.** Partner types a question (shown exactly). AI offers 3 replies with *different meanings* (not three wordings). The person: picks one, "None of these" (new meanings), "Guide my response" (agree, disagree, alternative, humor...), "Quick phrases" and "My words" (work with no AI), or "Start a conversation". **Primary input: both eyebrows up 500 ms = NEXT (one step per raise; holding longer never repeats); calibrated long blink = SELECT** (the item highlighted when the eyes closed; ordinary blinks ignored). **Safety:** select opens a review of the exact sentence that **starts on Cancel**; Speak needs one raise to reach Speak plus a **separate, new blink**. One gesture can never do two things. A gesture arbiter freezes the highlight on blink closure and discards ambiguous input. Losing the face pauses ("Tracking lost - Paused"); resume needs a deliberate blink. Fallbacks, all through the same review+confirm path: auto-scan + blink, keyboard/switch, touch. Consent-based learning: after 3 explicit "shorter" choices it asks; saved only after two separate approvals; viewable/pausable/deletable.

**Privacy / architecture.** Face tracking = MediaPipe FaceLandmarker (`@mediapipe/tasks-vision` 1.1.1, Apache-2.0 model served from our own origin) running locally in Safari; outputs only brow/eye-closure scores and a face count; not gaze, identity, or emotion; no frames recorded. Say **"by design, camera frames stay on the iPad"**, not "verified". With consent, Live AI sends the partner question, a few recent turns, and guidance to OpenAI Responses `gpt-6-astra` (server side, strict JSON schema, `store: false`, no tools; abuse-monitoring logs may be kept up to 30 days; no zero-retention claim). Only the exact confirmed sentence goes to ElevenLabs `eleven_v4_turbo` (Text-to-Dialogue WebSocket, server-side key, prebuilt voice Chris, not a clone; labeled basic-device-voice fallback). Backend on Railway (`https://api-production-18038.up.railway.app`), access code gates paid calls.
