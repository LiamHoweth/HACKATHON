# How to use Still Here (slide)

**Title:** Eyebrows, jaw, blink. One conversation.

- **Start:** open the app, tap one big Start button. Calibration is about 1 minute the first time and saved after that.
- **Partner just talks.** Speech is transcribed automatically and becomes the "Partner asked" line. Nothing to tap.
- **Likely replies** appear a few seconds later: the 2 most likely, then **Other ways to answer…** (3-5 directions like Rough day, Mixed day, Not now, You first). The top reply is already highlighted.
- **Long blink speaks it** in a natural voice, right away. A long blink while it is speaking stops it.
- **Eyebrows up moves**; keep them up to keep moving, drop your jaw briefly to go back. Move to another reply, **Other ways to answer…**, "None of these" (new suggestions), Guide my response, Quick phrases, or My words.
- **Hands-free listening** pauses while Still Here speaks. If the partner says more within about 20 s, it joins the same question and the suggestions refresh, without pulling the person out of a menu. Pure filler like "uh huh" or "mm-hmm" alone is ignored; short answers like "yeah" or "okay" count.
- **Safe by design:** nothing is said unless the person chooses it. Pause button and face-lost auto-pause. Basic device voice as a fallback.
- **Private:** camera images stay on the iPad and are never recorded. Only the partner's speech is sent for transcription.

| Gesture | What happens |
|---|---|
| Eyebrows up (about 0.35-0.5 s) | Next choice |
| Keep eyebrows up | Keep moving (a step every 0.6 s) |
| Brief jaw drop (about 0.4-1.5 s) | Back one choice |
| Long blink (about 1 s) | Select and speak |
| Long blink while speaking | Stop |
| Normal blinks | Ignored |
| Face lost, then long blink on Resume | Continue |

Typeface: Atkinson Hyperlegible, made for low-vision legibility. Other ways to answer, hold-to-move, jaw drop and the typeface are [deployed, unit-tested; NOT YET on iPad].

*Engineering prototype. Not a medical device. Works on iPad (native app) and in a browser.*
