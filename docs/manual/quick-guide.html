Still Here: Quick Guide

AI offers possible words. The person decides what to say.

Hold a conversation using only eyebrows and blinks. Runs as an iPad app and in a browser.

1. Start (helper, about 1 minute)

  1. Open Still Here. Landscape, at eye level, 50-70 cm from the face, with light on the face.
  2. First time only: iPadOS asks for camera and microphone. Tap Allow. Volume up, silent mode off.
  3. The launch card says "Ready when you are" and shows the defaults: eyebrows to move, long blink to choose; speaks as soon as you choose; live AI suggestions, natural voice.
  4. Tap the big Start button:
    • Start with saved calibration if the person is already calibrated.
    • Otherwise Next: set up eyebrow + blink control. Short calibration: relax, raise eyebrows a few times, practice, natural blinks, long deliberate blinks, review timing, practice selecting.
  5. Other ways to choose (auto-scan with blink, keyboard or switch, touch) are under Other ways to choose. Privacy, context and profile are under More options.

2. The two gestures

Long blink (about 1 s, deliberate)Select: the reply is spoken right away
Long blink while it is speakingStop speaking
Normal blinkingNothing. Ignored.
Face lost, look back, long blink on ResumeContinue after a pause

On screen: Eyebrows up 0.5 s: Next. Long blink: Select.

3. Having a conversation

  1. Partner: just talk near the iPad. What you say is transcribed and becomes the "Partner asked" line automatically.
  2. A few seconds later, three possible replies appear. The top one is already highlighted.
  3. Person: one long blink speaks it in a natural voice. Or raise eyebrows to move to another reply, None of these: new suggestions, Guide my response, Quick phrases, My words, or More tools.
  4. Listening pauses while Still Here speaks, then resumes. If the partner says more within about 20 s ("Are you coming to dinner?" then "It's at seven"), it joins the same question and the suggestions refresh, highlight back on top; the person is not pulled out of menus. Lines heard while Still Here speaks or a dialog is open wait and are answered right after. After about 20 s, a new line starts a new question.
  5. Pure filler alone ("uh huh", "mm-hmm", "hmm", "got it") is ignored. Short answers like "yeah", "okay" or "sure", and "Yes?", "No." and "Hey", still count.

What is on the screen

Left: the conversation

  • Big "Partner asked" text
  • 3 suggestion cards, top one highlighted
  • None of these: new suggestions
  • Action row: Guide my response, Quick phrases, My words, More tools

Right: camera and chat

  • Live camera preview (not recorded) and a gesture feedback line
  • Conversation panel with chat bubbles
  • "Listening: just talk" and a Stop listening button
  • Type or record instead for typing or Talk

Good to know

  • Fast mode (default): one long blink speaks immediately. Turn it off in setup to add a review step and a separate confirming blink. In fast mode, suggested sentences may be sent to the voice service ahead of time so the chosen one plays instantly.
  • Several faces: tracking follows the most central face that has been in view longest. Others are ignored.
  • Helper controls: Pause (top right). More tools, Controls, Voice switches to the basic device voice. The helper can always tap answers directly.

Privacy

  • Camera images stay on the iPad. Only eyebrow and eye movement is measured. Nothing is recorded.
  • The partner's speech is sent for transcription to make suggestions.
  • Suggestions are possibilities. Nothing is said unless the person chooses it.
Still Here is an engineering prototype. It is not a medical device.
