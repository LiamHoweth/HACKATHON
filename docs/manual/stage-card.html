Still Here: Stage Card

AI offers possible words. The person decides what to say.

1. PARTNER (talk only)

Stand close. Speak normally. One sentence. A follow-up within ~20 s merges into the same question. Don't count aloud.
Beat 1 (0:15)
"Are you coming to dinner tonight?"
Beat 2 (0:50) after the voice stops
"What time should we meet?"
Beat 3 (1:15) then silent
"Let's skip dinner and just stay in. Okay?"
Beat 4 (1:55) someone leans in
"Can I get you anything to drink?"
Beat 5 (2:25) open-ended
"How was your day?"
Open-ended questions can take up to ~8 s. Stay silent and wait.
Noisy hall or wrong transcript? Helper taps Stop listening. Type your line under "Type or record instead".
