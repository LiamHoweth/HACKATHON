# Still Here: quick guide

**AI offers possible words. The person decides what to say.**
Still Here lets someone who can't speak or move their hands hold a conversation using only their eyebrows and blinks.

## Before you start (helper, about 1 minute)
1. Open the **Still Here** app on the iPad and hold the iPad in landscape at eye level, 50–70 cm from the face, with light on the face.
2. If iPadOS asks for the **camera** or **microphone**, tap **Allow**. Make sure the volume is up.
3. On the setup screen, leave the defaults: **eyebrows + blinks**, **Speak as soon as I choose**, natural voice.
4. Tap **Start with saved calibration**. The first time, tap **Next** and follow the short calibration instead: relax, raise your eyebrows, then blink long a few times.

## Gestures

| Do this | It does this |
|---|---|
| **Raise both eyebrows** (hold about ½ second) | Move to the next choice |
| **Long blink** (close your eyes about 1 second) | Choose it. A suggestion is spoken right away |
| Normal blinking | Nothing. It is ignored |
| Long blink while it is speaking | Stop |

## Having a conversation
- **Partner:** just talk to the person, close to the iPad. The panel on the right shows "Listening — just talk", then what it heard.
- About 3 seconds later, **three possible replies** appear, and the **top one is already highlighted**.
- **Person:** one long blink speaks it. Or raise your eyebrows to move to another reply, **None of these** (new ideas), **Guide my response** (agree, disagree, ask, and so on), **Quick phrases** or **My words** (spell it out).
- Keep talking back and forth. Anything the partner says while the app is speaking is answered as soon as it finishes.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Face lost — paused" | Look back at the screen, then do one long blink on **Resume**. |
| Gestures are not picked up | Add light to the face, move a little closer, or recalibrate from setup. Glasses with glare can confuse it. |
| No sound | Turn up the volume and turn off silent mode. **More tools → Controls → Voice** switches to the basic device voice. |
| A noisy room picks up other voices | The partner taps **Stop listening** and types under **Type or record instead**. |
| Anything else | The helper taps **Pause**, and can also tap answers directly. |

## Privacy
- The camera image stays on the iPad. It only measures eyebrow and eye movement, and nothing is recorded.
- The partner's speech is sent for transcription only to make the suggestions. Listening pauses while the app speaks.
- The suggestions are possibilities. Nothing is said unless the person chooses it.
